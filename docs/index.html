Method
  • NISAR GCOV L-band scenes, orbit 118 ascending, frame 165, five dates.
  • Area of about 60 by 63 km at 20 m pixels; 5 x 5 speckle filter.
  • Change = HH backscatter of each date minus HH of 10 June 2026, in dB.
  • Zones: change above 3 dB and area of at least 0.1 km².
  • Main zones checked against Sentinel-2 optical images.
Limits
  • Five dates from winter to spring cannot separate a trend from the seasonal cycle.
  • A change in radar signal does not say by itself what changed.
  • No field validation; thresholds are provisional.
  • Radar sees the surface: no groundwater, no water quality.
  • This tool describes change. It does not assess impacts or assign responsibilities.

Data: NISAR (NASA/JPL and ISRO) via the Alaska Satellite Facility DAAC; Sentinel-2 (ESA, Copernicus). Built by Team Satellite Miner for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge "Dancing with the SARs". Not endorsed by NASA.
